## Description

Press play. Just listen.

ATPlayer is a simple player for your local audio files. Add your music, put the tracks in the order you want, and press play. Made for listening to the music you love without picking a new track every minute.

Your music comes first. You choose what plays. No recommendations, account, or music catalog.

With ATPlayer, you can:
• Add audio files and folders through the Files app.
• Create playlists, reorder tracks, and remove them from a playlist while keeping the original files.
• Play available local files without an internet connection.
• Keep listening in the background and control playback from the Lock Screen.
• See the current track and playback controls in Live Activities on supported devices.
• Adjust the sound with a 10-band equalizer and preamp.
• Use a layout that adapts to iPhone, iPad, and light or dark appearance.

Bring your own audio files. Music is not included. Files stored in iCloud Drive may need to be downloaded over the internet. The player keeps references to the files you select, so they need to remain accessible in their original location.
