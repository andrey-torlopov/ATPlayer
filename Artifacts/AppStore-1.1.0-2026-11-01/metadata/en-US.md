## Description

Press play. Just listen.

ATPlayer plays your own audio files on iPhone and iPad. Build playlists or open a folder of music and press play. Available local files can be played offline.

No ads, account, usage analytics, or tracking. The app does not collect data for the developer or send them your audio files, listening history, or library information. Playlists, settings, and file-access permissions stay on your device.

Playlists and folders
• Create and rename playlists, and add your audio through the system Files picker.
• Drag to reorder playlists and tracks. The current track keeps playing from the same position.
• Switch to Folders mode, browse subfolders, and play music without adding it to a playlist.
• Folder playback includes only audio files in the current directory. Browsing elsewhere does not change the queue already playing.
• Only selected root folders are remembered between launches. Contents are read again, and branches without supported audio are hidden.
• Importing a folder into a playlist also adds audio from its subfolders.

Playback
• Play in order, repeat the current playlist or folder, shuffle, or repeat one track.
• Keep listening in the background and control playback from the Lock Screen.
• See the current track and controls in Live Activities on supported devices.
• Adjust the sound with a 10-band equalizer and preamp.
• Use layouts for iPhone and iPad, with light and dark appearance.

Supports DRM-free MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, AIFF, FLAC, ALAC, and CAF files when their audio codec is supported by the device. Music is not included.

Local files can play without the internet. Reading directories and downloading audio from iCloud Drive, network storage, or other Files providers may require a network connection. Folders mode prepares audio from the open directory in a temporary local cache, prioritizing the selected track. The cache is cleared automatically and is not a permanent offline library. Playlists keep references to the originals, so those files must remain accessible.

Removing an entry from the player does not delete the original audio file. Playlist export creates JSON containing names and file paths, not audio recordings.

The app interface is currently in Russian.

Your files. Your order. Press play and listen.
